## Motivation and design

The chair reference worked, while early real-can inputs collapsed or stayed blurred. The August sequence explored masks and framing, intrinsics, free versus constrained axis/center, flow and alpha losses, geometry updates, and rotation direction. This is a reconstructed development sequence; several settings and code paths changed together.

## Findings and decision

The corrected no-flow positive-direction run recovered readable labels, and its continuation became the Nice Can milestone. R001 is the reference; failed, blocked-resume, and diagnostic attempts remain part of the history. Historical scores have their original protocols and cannot establish a ranking against later captures.
